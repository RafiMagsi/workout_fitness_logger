# Reel plan

This app is designed to be filmed as a fast social clip: one tap to log, immediate visual payoff, and a polished dark interface.

## Story beats

1. Open the app to the home screen showing the activity rings and a small chart already present.
2. Tap the + button and choose an exercise type.
3. Fill in duration and value quickly.
4. Save the entry.
5. Cut to the progress or home screen and show the chart/rings reacting.
6. Optional: switch the metric and log again to reinforce that the app is built for repeated use.

## Shot list

- Straight-on phone shot with a clean dark background.
- Tight framing so the rings and chart are readable.
- Close-up of the log screen for the quick fill moment.
- Close-up of the chart update after saving.
- Optional wider shot of the phone on a desk with soft light for a more cinematic feel.

## Caption ideas

- One tap to log, one screen to see progress.
- Small effort, visible result.
- Fitness tracking that stays out of the way.
